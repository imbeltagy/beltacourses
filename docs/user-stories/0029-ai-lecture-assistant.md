# US-0029 — AI Lecture Assistant

- **Phase:** Phase 5 — AI
- **Tasks:** T-031
- **Status:** Pending

## Story

As a student, I want to ask an AI assistant about the lecture I am watching, so that I can get a
summary, a hint or an explanation without leaving the course.

## Details

- Scoped to the lecture the student is on: ask questions, summarize it, get hints, explain a point.
- Answers are grounded in the lecture's own content.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A student who owns the course can open the assistant on a lecture.
- [ ] The assistant can summarize, hint and explain based on the lecture content.
- [ ] Usage is attributed to the student, with limits/quotas in place.
