Transactional emails are localized.

### Language

Taken from the language the user's app sends. Supported: **English** and **Arabic** (right-to-left).
Unknown or missing falls back to English.

### Sending

- A failed email never fails the action that triggered it.

### Used by

- Creating an account by staff emails the new user.
- Reset, verification and login-notification emails exist but wait for their features.
