An academy has an **owner** and, optionally, **moderators**.

### Owner

The person who created the academy. Only the owner:

- manages the academy's plan and billing;
- withdraws from the **academy wallet**;
- invites and removes moderators.

### Moderators

- Moderators are academy accounts only: never students, teachers, admins or super admins.
- A moderator of one academy can be the owner or a moderator of another.
- They invite teachers (as the owner does), set teacher shares, publish and manage the academy's courses,
  and act on behalf of the academy in the course workflow (024).

### Teachers

- A teacher accepts or declines an invitation. An invitation may show the proposed profit share.
- An accepted teacher is linked to the academy. Removing the link means the teacher can't add courses to
  that academy again until invited again.
