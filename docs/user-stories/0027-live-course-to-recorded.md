# US-0027 — Publish a Finished Live Course as a Regular Course

- **Phase:** Phase 4 — Live Courses & Assignments
- **Tasks:** T-029
- **Status:** Pending

## Story

As a teacher or academy, I want to republish a finished live course as a regular course, so that the
recordings keep earning after the live run ends.

## Details

- After a live course ends, it can be converted to a regular course — the lecture videos already exist
  from the recorded sessions.
- The price may stay the same or be changed.
- Students who purchased the live run **do not pay again**; they keep access to the regular course.
- Other students must buy it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A finished live course can be published as a regular course, with its recordings as lectures.
- [ ] The republished course can carry a new price.
- [ ] Previous live-run buyers keep access without a new purchase.
- [ ] New students must purchase it normally.
