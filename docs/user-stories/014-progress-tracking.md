## Sequence

- The student marks a lecture complete to **unlock the next item**, across module boundaries.
- The first lecture is unlocked on purchase.
- A locked lecture can't be opened and its video/content isn't served.
- **Demo lectures** are outside the gate and stay open to everyone.

## Assignments

They sit in the same sequence. The student marks one complete to continue, **passing is not required**.

## Progress

Percentage per course = completed items / total items, tracked per student and per item. An owned course listing returns it.

Undoing a completion lowers the percentage, but an item already unlocked stays reachable.
