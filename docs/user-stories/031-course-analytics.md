## Sales

- Number of students who **own** the course, **purchases** and **refunds**.
- List of transactions (amount, currency, date, status, split) **without any buyer identity**.
- A refunded purchase still counts as a purchase but no longer as an owner.

## Engagement

Completions per lecture, in course/module/lecture order, ready to plot as a graph to show the drop-off point.

## Access

A teacher sees only his own courses. An admin needs the analytics permission to see any course.
