Lecture videos are the first **private** files: they are not shown to everyone like images.

### Private access

- Paid content must never be reachable by a link alone, and a link a buyer shares must stop working.
- Only students who own the course (and whose lecture is unlocked), the owning teacher, and authorized
  admins can watch. **Demo lectures** are open to everyone.
- **Must be done before any paid content can be uploaded.**

### Large files

Videos are large, so uploading must survive a bad connection and continue where it stopped. An upload
nobody finished within **2 hours** is discarded.
