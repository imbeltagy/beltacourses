## Staying signed in

|                                                 | Clients       | Moderators   |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Stay signed in without re-entering the password | 7 days        | 10 hours     |
| Credential refreshed in the background          | every 30 mins | every 5 mins |

### Clients

Cannot be signed out remotely. They stay signed in until their time runs out.

### Moderators

- Every sign-in is a **session** that staff can end remotely.
- Signing out ends only that session.
- When an admin's **permissions change** (their group changes, or their group's permissions change),
  all of that admin's sessions end and they must sign in again.
