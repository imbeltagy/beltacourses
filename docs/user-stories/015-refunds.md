A student requests a refund for a purchase, with a reason. An authorized reviewer approves or rejects it.

### On approval (one atomic operation)

- The amount is credited to the **student wallet**.
- The teacher's share is deducted from the **teacher wallet**.
- The platform's share is deducted from the **system wallet**.
- The course is removed from the student's owned list.

A rejected request changes nothing.

Whether the system fee is fully refunded is still undecided.
