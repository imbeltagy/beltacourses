An offer has a value, an optional date window and an optional usage limit, and may have a unique **coupon code** validated at checkout.

### Audience

**First-time students only** or **all students**.

### Who pays for the discount

- Created by the **system**: the teacher still gets his **full** amount, the platform absorbs the discount.
- Created by the **teacher**: he gets his profit **after** the discount.

The owner of each offer is recorded.
