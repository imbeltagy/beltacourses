### Responsibilities

- The **teacher** creates and updates the course.
- The **academy** accepts and publishes it, and may edit it on behalf of the teacher. A teacher alone can't publish an academy course.

### Price and split

The academy sets the **price** and the **teacher's share** per course.

The system takes its fixed share first (e.g. 20%), then the academy decides how the remaining 80% is divided between the academy and the teacher. A purchase credits the system, academy and teacher wallets accordingly.
