### Responsibilities

- The **teacher** creates the course and controls its content.
- The academy **owner or moderators** set the price, publish it, disable or remove it, and may edit it on
  behalf of the teacher. They can also create courses themselves.
- A teacher alone can't publish, close or delete an academy course. This holds whether or not the teacher
  is still in the academy: a removed teacher's existing courses stay with the academy.

### Price and split

The academy sets the **price**.

The system takes its fixed share first (e.g. 20%), then the academy decides how the remaining 80% is divided between the academy and the teacher. That division is one **share per teacher** that applies to all their courses in the academy, with no per-course override. A purchase credits the system, academy and teacher wallets accordingly. Changing a share affects future sales only.
